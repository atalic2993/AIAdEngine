# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-09-10*

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** AI Ad Engine helps South African businesses plan, launch and manage advertising across Facebook, Instagram, Google and TikTok, then track enquiries in one connected CRM and sales pipeline.

**What it does:** The platform guides customers from advertising research and campaign creation through launch, reporting, lead management and follow-up. AI assists with campaign structure, targeting ideas, advertising copy, creative direction and analysis. Customers remain responsible for their offer, budget, approvals and sales follow-up, with human guidance available according to their plan.

**Product category:** Advertising management software with AI-assisted campaign creation, CRM and pipeline tracking.

**Product type:** Subscription software with self-service and done-with-you service levels.

**Business model:**

- Scale: R599 per month, primarily self-service.
- Dominate: R1,599 per month, with additional setup, campaign and support assistance.
- Both plans are month-to-month.
- Advertising spend is separate and paid to the advertising platforms.
- Payment is completed through PayFast.
- Login details are emailed within one business day of payment.

## Target Audience

**Target companies:** Owner-led South African small and growing businesses that need a reliable flow of enquiries, bookings, quotations or online sales. Strong fits include local services, trades, professional services, health, beauty, fitness, property, automotive, retail and e-commerce.

**Decision-makers:** Business owners, founders and hands-on marketing managers.

**Primary use case:** Run digital advertising without mastering several advertising platforms or paying a full agency retainer, while keeping leads and follow-up visible after the click.

**Jobs to be done:**

- Turn a business offer into a structured advertising campaign.
- Manage several advertising channels from one place.
- Understand which campaigns produce enquiries and sales opportunities.
- Keep new leads organised and make follow-up easier.
- Get practical guidance without handing over all control.

**Use cases:**

- A local service business wants more calls, quotations or bookings in a defined service area.
- A retailer or e-commerce business wants to grow online sales across search and social channels.
- An owner already boosting posts wants more control and clearer reporting.
- A small marketing team wants advertising, lead management and reporting in one workflow.

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** Small businesses need digital advertising to produce customers, but the work is split across complicated tools and usually stops at click-level reporting.

**Why alternatives fall short:**

- Boosting posts offers limited control and teaches little about what produced a result.
- Native advertising dashboards require specialist knowledge and constant attention.
- Separate tools make it difficult to connect spend, enquiries and sales follow-up.
- Agency retainers can be too large relative to a small business's advertising budget.
- Agency turnaround can prevent owners from reacting quickly to offers or market changes.

**What it costs them:** Wasted advertising spend, missed enquiries, slow follow-up, unclear decisions and time taken away from running the business.

**Emotional tension:** Owners worry that they are wasting money, making the wrong changes or missing leads without knowing where the problem is.

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** Other AI-assisted advertising platforms that combine campaign creation and management. They may lack South African pricing, local business context or accessible human support.

**Secondary:** Meta Ads Manager, Google Ads and TikTok Ads Manager used directly. These provide full control but require customers to learn and manage separate systems.

**Secondary:** Traditional marketing agencies. They can provide broader strategy and full-service delivery, but may cost more, move more slowly and reduce the owner's day-to-day control.

**Indirect:** Boosted posts, freelancers, spreadsheets and doing nothing. These are familiar or inexpensive but do not create a connected, measurable advertising and lead-management workflow.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**

- Advertising across Facebook, Instagram, Google and TikTok from one product.
- AI assistance across research, campaign structure, copy, creative direction and analysis.
- Built-in CRM and sales pipeline for tracking enquiries after the click.
- Self-service and done-with-you options.
- Month-to-month pricing in Rands.
- Human support when customers need guidance.
- Customers keep ownership and control of their advertising accounts, assets, budgets and business decisions.

**How we do it differently:** AI Ad Engine connects campaign work, reporting and lead follow-up in one guided workflow instead of treating campaign launch as the finish line.

**Why that's better:** Customers can act faster, use fewer disconnected tools and make advertising decisions with more of the customer journey visible.

**Why customers choose us:** They want more help and clarity than native advertising tools provide, without the cost or loss of control that can come with a full agency arrangement.

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
| --- | --- |
| "I do not know enough about advertising." | The workflow guides each stage, AI assists with the work, and the Dominate plan provides more hands-on help. |
| "Is the advertising budget included?" | No. The subscription covers AI Ad Engine; advertising spend is paid separately to Meta, Google or TikTok. |
| "Will AI run everything for me?" | No. AI assists with the work, while the customer keeps control of the offer, budget, approvals and business decisions. |
| "Can you guarantee leads or sales?" | No. Results depend on the business, offer, market, budget, creative, targeting and sales follow-up. |
| "Is this a marketing agency?" | No. Scale is primarily self-service. Dominate adds hands-on help using the platform, but it is not positioned as a full-service agency retainer. |
| "Am I locked into a contract?" | No. Plans operate month-to-month and can be cancelled under the published policy. |

**Anti-persona:** Large or complex advertisers needing a dedicated team for brand strategy, production, media planning and multi-market account management. Agencies are not a primary audience because multi-client and white-label capabilities are not confirmed.

## Switching Dynamics

**Push:** Confusing dashboards, missed leads, unclear reporting, slow agency changes and retainers that consume too much of a smaller budget.

**Pull:** One guided workspace, pricing in Rands, month-to-month flexibility, built-in lead tracking and real human help.

**Habit:** Boosting posts, relying on familiar providers, keeping leads in WhatsApp or spreadsheets and postponing proper campaign setup.

**Anxiety:** Fear of wasting money, losing historical advertising data, setting up accounts incorrectly, learning unfamiliar software or being left without support.
